A live dashboard for the Sea to Sky Highway. Drive times, highway cameras, DriveBC warnings and reports from other drivers, from Vancouver to Pemberton, on one map.

What it is

One road, everything about it

Highway 99 is the only road between Vancouver, Squamish, Whistler and Pemberton. When it’s clear, it’s one of the best drives anywhere; when there’s a crash, a closure or snow, there’s no other way around. Finding out before you leave means checking DriveBC, the cameras, the weather and a Facebook group of locals, one tab at a time.

Highway 99 puts all of that on one map of the corridor. Every source refreshes every two minutes, so a glance tells you whether today is a good day to go, and when.

Why it’s useful

Know before you’re in the lineup

  • Where the delay is, not just how long

    Live drive times for each leg, from Downtown Vancouver to Pemberton, each compared with a normal day, so you can see which stretch is slow and which direction is faster.

  • Plan around it

    Pick where you’re starting and where you’re going, then choose a time to depart or a time to arrive, and get the other end worked out from current traffic.

  • See it for yourself

    Twenty-two highway cameras are pinned to the map and listed by direction, along with the speeds currently posted on DriveBC’s variable speed signs.

  • Hear it from other drivers

    DriveBC’s official events sit next to posts from the Sea to Sky road conditions group. Places named in a post are picked out and can be shown on the map.

How it works

A glance before you go

  1. 01

    Check the drive

    Drive times come from live traffic, leg by leg and colour-coded by how much slower than usual each one is. Flip between northbound and southbound, or open the planner for an arrival time.

  2. 02

    Look at the road

    Tap a camera on the map or in the list to see the latest image, from Lions Bay to Pemberton. A dotted line ties each one back to where it sits on the highway.

  3. 03

    Read what’s going on

    Construction, closures and incidents from DriveBC, marked by severity, then what drivers are reporting right now: a crash just past Lions Bay, a bear cub heading for the road.

Under the hood

How it’s built

Front end
React, TypeScript and Vite, with React Query and Zustand
Map
Mapbox GL with a custom live-traffic style, camera and speed-sign markers, and animated wind from Open-Meteo
Data
DriveBC’s Open511 events and variable speed signs, highway cameras, Environment Canada weather warnings, Open-Meteo forecasts and Mapbox traffic-aware driving times
API
One Express function on Vercel that caches each source for a few minutes
