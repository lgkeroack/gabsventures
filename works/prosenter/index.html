Prosenter

Is everyone following along?

A Windows app for anyone who presents by sharing their screen. It keeps a numbered title from your script on top of whatever you’re showing, so the audience always knows which part they’re watching.

  1. Import your script

    Drop in a CSV with three columns: number, title and speaker notes. The titles fill a list; the notes appear beside the one you’re on.

  2. Stage the title

    A placeholder shows your longest title. Drag and resize it to where it should sit, and pick the font, size, colour and outline.

  3. Present

    Start, and only the title stays, on top of everything. Around it the window is transparent, so clicks reach the demo underneath. Step forward and back as you go.

  4. Drive it from the keyboard

    Global shortcuts move between titles, pause and resume, and stop, even while the app you’re demoing has focus.

How a presentation runs: from a CSV to a title on screen.

What it is

A table of contents that follows you

Screen-share presentations lose people. Someone glances at another window, or joins late, and the software demo on screen no longer tells them where you are in the story. Slides have titles; a live demo doesn’t.

Prosenter adds them back. You write a short script of numbered sections, and while you present it shows the current one as an outlined title over the screen you’re sharing. Your notes for that section stay in Prosenter’s own window, off to the side.

Why it’s useful

Keep the room with you

  • Everyone knows where you are

    A numbered title on screen tells late arrivals and wandering attention exactly which part of the demo they’re in.

  • Works over anything

    The title floats above whatever you’re showing, with no window chrome, and stays out of the way of the app you’re clicking through.

  • Hands stay on the demo

    Next, previous, pause and stop are system-wide shortcuts, so you never have to switch windows mid-sentence. Pausing hides the title until you resume.

  • Readable on any background

    Titles are drawn with an outline in the font, size and colours you choose, so they stay legible over light and dark screens alike.

Under the hood

How it’s built

App
C# and WPF on .NET 6
Overlay
A borderless, transparent, always-on-top window with a custom outlined-text control
Shortcuts
Windows global hotkeys, which keep working while another app has focus
Script
A plain CSV of number, title and notes, imported from the menu or by drag and drop
Delivery
GitHub Actions publishes a single, self-contained Prosenter.exe for every release
