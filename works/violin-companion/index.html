Violin Companion

Am I in tune yet?

A practice app for violin and other bowed strings: a tuner, scales, a metronome, and a play-along game that listens while you play and scores every note.

What it is

A practice room in a browser tab

Practising a bowed instrument means juggling a tuner, a metronome, a scale book and sheet music, and you still can’t hear yourself as well as you think. Violin Companion puts all of it in one app that listens through the microphone.

The Workstation tab has the tools: tuner, tuning presets, scales on a fingerboard, a scale trainer, a metronome and an audio mixer. The Song Play tab turns practice into a game, in the spirit of Rocksmith: pick a tune, play along, and see exactly which notes were sharp, flat, early or late.

Why it’s useful

Honest feedback, every note

  • It hears what you play

    Pitch detection runs live on the microphone, or on a wired headset, USB mic or audio interface, and grades each note for pitch and timing.

  • It grows with you

    Learn a Song starts each phrase with just its key notes and adds more as you play it accurately. Riff Repeater loops the hard bit and speeds it up once you’ve got it.

  • Hundreds of tunes, any instrument

    251 built-in songs across twelve genres, a live search of about 23,000 traditional tunes on The Session, and MIDI or ABC import. Every song is re-arranged for violin, viola, cello, bass or fiddle tunings.

  • Works anywhere

    Install it from the browser or as an Android app. It works offline after the first visit and updates itself.

How it works

Tune, warm up, play

  1. 01

    Tune up

    Play a string and the tuner shows the note, the frequency and how many cents you’re off, and highlights the string you’re closest to. Thirteen presets run from violin and cello to fiddle cross-tunings, the Hardanger fiddle and the erhu.

  2. 02

    See the scale

    Choose a key and one of sixteen scales and the fingerboard lights up every note in it, with the root marked and the note you’re playing shown live. The scale trainer then checks you through it, note by note.

  3. 03

    Pick a song

    Browse by genre and level, see each song’s difficulty, tempo, key and positions, then choose how to play it: learn it, chase a high score, or loop the hardest phrases.

Under the hood

How it’s built

Songs
