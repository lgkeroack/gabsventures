About

The short version

I don't believe in picking one thing. The best work happens at the intersection of disciplines — where software meets physical craft, where engineering meets art, where building meets teaching.

I write code, weld steel, shape wood, and organize events. Sometimes all in the same week. Each discipline teaches me something the others can't.

My workshop has a lathe, a 3D printer, a server rack, and a table saw. They get along fine.
